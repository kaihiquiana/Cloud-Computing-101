# Amazon Web Services (AWS)

## Brief Overview
AWS was launched in 2006 and is the oldest and biggest cloud provider. It offers a huge number of services and is used by many companies from small startups to big enterprises.

## Global Infrastructure
AWS has 32 regions around the world, each with multiple availability zones. This means they have data centers in many countries, so services stay fast and available.

## Cloud Management Console
You can access AWS through their website at console.aws.amazon.com. It's a dashboard where you can find and manage all their services in one place.

## Four Core Services
1. **Amazon EC2** — lets you create and manage virtual servers.
2. **Amazon S3** — storage service where you can save files, photos, videos, and backups.
3. **Amazon VPC** — lets you set up a private network in the cloud.
4. **Amazon RDS** — managed database service so you don't have to maintain it yourself.

## Three Advantages
- They have the most services available compared to anyone else.
- They have a big community and lots of tutorials online.
- Their Free Tier is generous, which is good for students and startups.

## Common Uses
- Hosting websites and apps
- Storing large amounts of data
- Running business software that used to be on-premise
