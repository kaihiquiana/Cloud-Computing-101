# Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most. Even though AWS has more services, GCP felt cleaner and easier to understand. Their strengths in data and AI seem really powerful, and it makes sense because Google knows those areas best.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
All three work basically the same way. They all give you virtual computers, storage, and databases that you can set up online. The ideas are the same — you pay for what you use, scale up or down, and manage everything through a website. The differences are mostly in the details.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
It's not about which cloud is "best" overall — it's about what fits the business. If they already use Microsoft, choose Azure. If they do AI research, choose GCP. If they want the biggest community and most options, choose AWS. The technology should match what the business actually needs.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
I learned that choosing a cloud provider is mostly about listening first. You have to understand what the client already has and what they are trying to do. You can't just pick your favorite — you have to recommend what will actually work for them.

## 5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved?
Now my portfolio has a proper comparison of all three major clouds. It shows I understand not just one platform, but how they compare and when to use each. It looks organized and ready to show what I learned.
